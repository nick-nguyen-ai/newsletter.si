## Big picture
- Two fears pulled against each other this week: money left debt-funded AI stocks for bonds, while oil climbed back towards US$110 as Gulf attacks spread.
- AMP's My Bui reads the bond rally as investors now fearing an AI bust more than inflation ([AMP](https://www.amp.com.au/resources/insights-hub/weekly-market-update-16-10-2026-amp)).
- Saturday's news added to the oil risk rather than easing it.

## Macro and geopolitics
- **Hormuz:** Iran's Revolutionary Guards say a supertanker carrying more than 2 million barrels hit a mine and caught fire on Saturday. The US says the mines are cleared and ships are being hit by Iranian projectiles ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/17/super-tanker-ablaze-after-striking-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz-irgc-says), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/us-and-iran-both-claim-control-of-strait-of-hormuz-amid-attacks-on-vessels)).
- **How much oil gets through:** Kpler says about two-thirds of pre-war flows. US Central Command says 100 million barrels passed last week. The UK monitor says most attacked ships no longer report, so the true count is likely higher ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/us-and-iran-both-claim-control-of-strait-of-hormuz-amid-attacks-on-vessels), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-yemen-houthis-saudi-arabia/)).

- **Peace talks:** Iran says it sent Washington, via Qatar, a seven-day plan to reopen the strait if its conditions are met: "The choice is now America's" ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610179732)). No breakthrough is expected soon, and US media report Trump has told the military to be ready for bigger strikes after the 3 November midterms ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/us-and-iran-both-claim-control-of-strait-of-hormuz-amid-attacks-on-vessels)).
- **A second chokepoint:** The Houthis struck Aramco's Dhahran headquarters and now hold the Bab el-Mandeb, the main bypass for Gulf oil ([CBC/Reuters](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/saudi-arabia-aramco-houthis-missile-9.7379854)). Aramco's shipping kept running ([TradeWinds](https://www.tradewindsnews.com/tankers/aramco-shipping-keeps-rolling-after-houthi-strike-on-dhahran-headquarters/2-1-2059031)).
- **What it means here:** Supports ASX energy stocks, but keeps inflation and airfares high.

- **Trump and Russia:** Trump suspended the Graham Act sanctions and tariffs on buyers of Russian energy days before they started, to protect his diesel deal with Putin ([Investing.com](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/trump-suspends-sweeping-russia-tariffs-015245423.html)). That lifts the threat of huge US tariffs on China and India, a quiet plus for Australia's biggest customer, but angered both parties in Congress ([AP via PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trumps-decision-to-waive-russian-sanctions-outrages-congress-but-no-quick-counter-seen)). AMP notes the diesel covers only about a day of world demand.
- **Midterms:** Polls point to Democrats winning the House, and Reuters reports Trump is blaming his aides ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/politics-news/trump-faults-aides-as-republican-midterm-prospects-dim-4955376)).
- **Ukraine:** One of Russia's biggest attacks yet on Ukraine's power grid, and Putin says a "security zone" inside Ukraine isn't negotiable ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-attacks-kill-7-injure-dozens-across-ukraine-amid-continued-strikes-on-energy-infrastructure/), [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/ukraine-russia-war-live-trump-putin-zelensky-starlink-b3068450.html)). Peace looks further off, which keeps diesel tight.
- **Bonds:** US 10-year yields fell despite hot inflation, as money fled AI stocks and Fed officials signalled no rush ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/most-jgbs-rise-tracking-gains-in-u-s-treasurys-80602aa7)). Traders expect a Fed hold on 28 October and a hike in December ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/australian-dollar-pares-gains-as-us-dollar-steadies-rba-hike-bets-ease-202610161740)).
- **RBA:** Australian yields fell further after September's surprise drop in jobs. Markets give a November hike only about a one-in-eight chance ([Westpac](https://library.westpaciq.com.au/content/dam/public/westpaciq/secure/economics/documents/aus/2026/10/WBC_MorningReport16Oct2026.pdf)).

## Where analysts differ
- **RBA in November:** Westpac still expects a hike because inflation risks dominate. AMP expects a hold with tough talk ([Westpac Weekly](https://library.westpaciq.com.au/content/dam/public/westpaciq/secure/economics/documents/aus/2026/10/WestpacWeekly20261019.pdf), [AMP](https://www.amp.com.au/resources/insights-hub/weekly-market-update-16-10-2026-amp)).
- **Bear on shares:** Longview Economics' Chris Watling expects a "big, chunky sell-off" of 10 to 20% in US shares as tighter liquidity hits credit, though a mid-cycle correction rather than a recession ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/market-correction-chris-watling.html)). AMP flags CoreWeave's bonds at distressed levels and the risk of contagion through credit.
- **Bull on shares:** AMP still likes shares over 12 months. It argues the AI spending boom can run another two to three years and that investment-grade credit, where most big tech borrowers sit, is calm ([AMP](https://www.amp.com.au/resources/insights-hub/weekly-market-update-16-10-2026-amp)).
